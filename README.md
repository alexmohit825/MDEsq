## 🏛️ Executive Summary
**MDEsq** is an executive-grade native iOS application engineered for Dr. A. Alex Mohit and licensed healthcare professionals. Built purely in **SwiftUI (iOS 18+)**, MDEsq provides offline contract redlining, voice-enabled malpractice precedent searching, bad-faith sham peer review diagnostics, trial deposition masterclass training, and Stark Law Fair Market Value (FMV) compensation benchmarks.

## 📱 Architecture & Key Native Modules
- **`MDEsqApp.swift` / `MainTabView.swift`**: Native iOS 18 tab navigation, custom executive color palette, and fluid transitions.
- **`ContractAnalyzerView.swift`**: On-device physician contract audit engine detecting 7 predatory trap clauses (RCW 49.62 non-competes, 100% tail insurance, uncompensated emergency call) with verbatim replacement redlines.
- **`PrecedentFinderView.swift`**: Native `SFSpeechRecognizer` voice dictation matching clinical complications against WA, OR, CA, and NY malpractice caselaw with operative defensive charting formulas.
- **`PeerReviewView.swift`**: 10-Factor Sham Peer Review Retaliation Index, dynamic risk gauge, and HCQIA 30-day summary suspension playbook.
- **`DepositionMasterclassView.swift`**: 8 Cardinal Deposition Rules and Reptile Theory cross-examination counter-scripts.
- **`FMVCalculatorView.swift`**: Dynamic compensation percentile calculator and Stark Law commercial reasonableness verification across 13 medical/surgical subspecialties.
- **`Assets.xcassets`**: 1024×1024 bespoke luxury brushed gold & emerald app icon.
