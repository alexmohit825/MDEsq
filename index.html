Contract Clause Red Flag Scanner

Audit restrictive covenants, tail, and termination

Non-Competition Covenant RCW 49.62 Protected

In Washington (RCW 49.62), non-competes are void unless the physician earns >$120,559/yr (adjusted annually). Cannot exceed 18 months, and moonlighting prohibitions are restricted. If hospital tries to enforce an invalid covenant, physician gets statutory damages + attorney fees.

Tail Insurance Obligation Negotiate 100% Employer

Claims-made policies require extended reporting coverage ("tail"). Never agree to pay 100% of tail if terminated without cause or due to hospital material breach. Demand hospital covers 100% or 50/50 mutual split.

Without-Cause Notice Period Target 90–120 Days

30-day notice is hazardous for surgical subspecialties (requires 90+ days for hospital credentialing and surgical transition). Ensure mutual notice parity with garden leave or continuation of pay.

Unilateral Amendment Trap Strike / Require Written Consent

Reject clauses allowing hospital to modify compensation methodologies, call expectations, or clinical hours via "updates to medical staff bylaws or administrative policy" without signed mutual consent.

State Medical Board Investigation Defense Protocol

Washington Medical Commission (WMC) & Uniform Disciplinary Act (RCW 18.130)

1 Phase 1: Notice of Complaint & Letter of Cooperation Critical Window (14–30 Days)

The Cardinal Rule: Never draft or submit an initial response unrepresented or without first securing the complete certified medical record and audit trail. Initial physician statements are binding and cannot be retracted.

2 Phase 2: Case Management Committee (CMC) Review Threshold Determination

The panel of commissioners evaluates investigator reports and expert consultant reviews. Three outcomes: (1) Closure with no action, (2) Letter of Concern (non-disciplinary educational notice), (3) Authorization of formal charges.

3 Phase 3: Stipulation to Informal Disposition (STID) NPDB Protection Shield

Under RCW 18.130.172, an informal disposition (STID) does NOT constitute a finding of unprofessional conduct and is generally NOT reportable to the National Practitioner Data Bank (NPDB) unless it involves a restriction on clinical practice.

4 Phase 4: Statement of Charges & OAH Administrative Hearing Formal Trial

If charges are filed, hearing is conducted by an Administrative Law Judge (ALJ) from the Office of Administrative Hearings (OAH). Standard of proof in Washington is Clear and Convincing Evidence for physician license revocation.

NPDB Mandatory Reporting Matrix

  • 🚨 Reportable: • Hospital suspension >30 days resulting from peer review
    • Any monetary medical malpractice settlement/verdict
    • Voluntary surrender of privileges while under investigation
  • 🛡️ Non-Reportable: • Hospital precautionary suspension <30 days
    • WMC Letter of Concern (non-disciplinary)
    • Hospital peer review remediation with no privilege loss
